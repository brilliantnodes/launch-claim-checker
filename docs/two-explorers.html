Two explorers, a bond and a challenge

Launch Claim Checker v2

← The free checker · Claim bounties

A launch claim such as "no founder reward" is checked on two block explorers on different domains. Each GenLayer validator reads both pages itself. A verdict stands only when both pages show the same thing; when they disagree, the answer is "the explorers disagree".

  1. Check. Anyone names the coin, the claim and two block-1 pages (or two mining pages). Free, apart from the network fee.
  2. Bond. Within the bond window the requester can put test GEN behind a "no premine" or "no founder reward" verdict that both explorers agree on about a coin's block 0 or 1 (rules BOTH:P1 or BOTH:P2). A verdict that the block pays a founder allocation (BOTH:P1) can be bonded only when both pages also show the block's ordinary reward, not zero: a corrected page can then be read the other way.
  3. Challenge. While the challenge window is open, anyone else can deposit the same amount to say the verdict is wrong.
  4. Re-check. After a short grace period anyone can have the validators read both pages again. The same fact again: upheld, the requester takes the challengers' deposits. The opposite verdict about the same block: overturned, half the bond is burned and the challengers share the rest, so challenging yourself from other wallets does not pay. The explorers now showing opposite facts, a definite reading of another block or amount, or no decided re-check in time: not confirmed, everyone gets their own money back. A reading where a page cannot be read or shows nothing that decides (an error page) decides nothing, and the re-check window stays open. A bond nobody challenges is released.

Read this first. A check trusts the two pages: whoever runs both explorers can decide what they show. Two domains make that harder, not impossible. A verdict says what the pages showed when they were read; it is not a finding about the coin. Test GEN only.

    Limits

    • Two pages run by the same party are not two witnesses. The contract only requires two different domains; it cannot tell who runs them.
    • All four rules are a model's reading with code checks, not a proof: code checks that every amount, block number and miner name the model gives is printed on that validator's own copy of the page.
    • Only "no premine" and "no founder reward" verdicts about a coin's block 0 or 1 can be bonded: only there can the opposite reading exist, so a verdict on a later block could never be overturned. A mining verdict changes from block to block, and "fair launch" is never supported (so its bond could never be overturned); neither can be bonded.
    • A re-check reads the pages as they are then: a page that changed decides the outcome; that is the point of a re-check, and also its limit. A page that cannot be read, does not show the coin or shows nothing that decides (an error page) decides nothing, so whoever controls one of the two explorers can keep a re-check from deciding, and the bond then ends not confirmed. The first decided re-check is final, so a correction has to be on both explorers when the re-check window opens.
    • When the verdict is undetermined, only each page's class and rule are stored: no amount or line that the validators did not all see.
    • Refused bonds and challenges are credited straight back; withdraw them any time. Withdrawals reach the wallet when the transaction is finalized.
    • The network follows redirects without saying so. Use pages that do not redirect.
