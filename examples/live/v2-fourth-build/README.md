# Live records of the fourth v2 build (superseded)

These records are from the build of `contracts/launch_claim_checker_v2.py` at commit cb4261e (the fixes after review 4: a `BOTH:P1` verdict can be bonded only when both pages also show the block's ordinary reward), run on 26 Nov 2026, on Studio and on Testnet Bradbury Phase 3. Review 5 ([`reviews/v2/README.md`](../../../reviews/v2/README.md), section 5) then reviewed this build and these runs, after the runs, and found that the other validators accepted the leader's ordinary reward without checking it against their own reading, so one faulty or dishonest leader could still make such a verdict bondable on a block whose correction could never read as P2; and that what was kept as the reward was looser than needed. Nothing like it happened in these runs: every stored reward was the model's own line and the block's real reward. The contract was fixed again (commit b4966bf), deployed again, and the live runs were repeated; the current records are the ones in `examples/live/` (see [`MILESTONE-2.md`](../../../MILESTONE-2.md)).

These are kept unchanged as a record of what was run; to verify them, check out commit 054ffd8 (the contract, builds and tools they were made with) and run `tools/verify_v2.py` and `tools/verify_v2_bradbury.py` there; both report 0 mismatches.

- `v2-studio.json`: bonds and challenges on Studio `0x30B1E354AD5056A9Ba7acDE01b277aF1C24358e7`; 45 of 45 transactions as expected, 12 of 12 checks.
- `v2-site-studio.json`: the page clicked through on Studio `0x11630CbaC8dbaD37b76Dc3aa0dEfF46C03361445`; 8 of 8.
- `v2-bradbury.json`: the same stories on Bradbury Phase 3 `0xDB8f4cF9824D1E41Fdb1C252a09BaDA6b45f4F52`; 44 of 44, every one agreed by all five validators. Two notes after the run: step 21 (a re-check sent too early) was refused as intended but marked otherwise by a slip in the run tool, since fixed; and the balance, read right after the withdrawals, matched once they were finalized (the run read it at 10:13:48 UTC; a read every 30 seconds after the run first showed 50000000000000000 wei at 10:43:04 UTC, and the explorer shows the three withdrawals finalized at 10:42:14 to 10:42:47 UTC).
